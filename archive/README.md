# Archive: material no longer published

Files here were part of the guide and are no longer on the site. They stay in the repository, with their history, because they were sourced work and because the decision to stop publishing them is reversible.

Archived on 23 August 2026 under decision D038. The test applied: a library page stays only if an architecture page cites it or a published skill bundles it. Two kinds of material failed that test.

**Volatile vendor research.** The vendor hub (`vendors/`) and the per-layer vendor tables (`research/R*/vendors.md`) carried dated product facts across ten profiles, a coverage matrix and fourteen layer tables. Product names, packaging, meters and general-availability dates in this category change within a quarter, and the guide cannot re-verify that surface at the rate it moves. The one-page wall chart keeps naming representative products for orientation, and it is re-verified on a two-monthly cycle instead.

**Methodology instruments.** The roadmap checklist, the vendor question bank and the vendor scorecard were process artifacts rather than architecture. The roadmap checklist's spine and its entry and exit gates were absorbed into the first-agent path and day-one floor pages before it was archived; the disqualifiers that mattered in the question bank survive as the "stop buying" lines on the layer pages and as the decision catalog. Research briefs (`research/R*/brief.md`) were scoping documents written to start each track, never reader content.

Nothing here is maintained. Dates, prices, product names and availability statements were accurate when written and have not been checked since. Do not cite this material as current. The published guide is at https://www.agenticarchitectureskills.com and its evidence lives in the research tracks' findings and sources.
