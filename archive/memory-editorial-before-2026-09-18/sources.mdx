## What the benchmarks measure

- LoCoMo: arXiv 2402.17753 (27 February 2024); ACL 2024 long papers, pages 13851 to 13870; github.com/snap-research/locomo, file task_eval/evaluation.py (code comments assigning category 1 to multi-hop); project page snap-research.github.io/locomo. Token counts: 9K in the arXiv v1 abstract, 16K in the ACL abstract, about 26K measured in arXiv 2504.19413 [vendor].
- The 2025 dispute: blog.getzep.com, "Is Mem0 really SOTA in agent memory?" (6 May 2025, modified 3 June 2026; correction to 75.14 plus or minus 0.17) [vendor]; github.com/getzep/zep-papers issue 5 (8 May 2025, recomputation to 58.44, filed by Mem0's chief technology officer) [vendor]; arXiv 2504.19413 tables 1 and 2 (full-context 72.90 against 68.44) [vendor].
- Later LoCoMo claims, all on the 1,540-question subset: getzep.com/research (94.7, reader and judge gpt-5.4, modified 27 August 2026) [vendor]; mem0.ai/research and docs.mem0.ai memory evaluation (92.5, managed platform, 12 September 2026) [vendor]; byterover.dev benchmark post (92.2, 27 February 2026) [vendor]; arXiv 2512.12818 (89.61) [vendor].
- LoCoMo label audit: dev.to, Vektor, "Is the industry standard test broken?" (22 April 2026; 99 score-corrupting labels, 45 of 47 commonsense answers undefined) [vendor, claims checkable against the released data]; ATANT, arXiv 2604.10981 (13 April 2026; empty-gold scoring bug affecting 23 percent of the corpus).
