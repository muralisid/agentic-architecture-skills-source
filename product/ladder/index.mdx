An agent’s result depends on its instructions, information, tools, workflow, and model. Start with a real failure and identify which part needs to change. More training is useful only when it addresses the actual limitation.

<TeachingIllustration src="/figures/ladder/capability-choice.svg" mobileSrc="/figures/ladder/capability-choice-mobile.svg" title="Choose the change that fits the problem" alt="Observe a failure: Use a real task. Locate the cause: Information, tool, model. Try a small change: Keep a matched baseline. Measure the result: Quality, time, cost. Release or revise: Keep the ability to undo" caption="More training is one option. A better tool or clearer evidence may solve the same problem." />

## Improve how the agent works

<Cards>
<Card title="Instructions" href="/ladder/instructions">Clarify the task, constraints, and expected result.</Card>
<Card title="Context" href="/ladder/context">Supply the right evidence and current task state.</Card>
<Card title="Tools and the loop" href="/ladder/tools-and-the-loop">Let the agent gather evidence, act within limits, and inspect the result.</Card>
</Cards>

## Adapt the model when the task justifies it

<Cards>
<Card title="Adapters and fine-tuning" href="/ladder/adapters-and-fine-tuning">Teach a repeatable behavior using suitable examples.</Card>
<Card title="Distillation" href="/ladder/distillation">Transfer a demonstrated capability to a smaller or cheaper model.</Card>
<Card title="Reinforcement fine-tuning" href="/ladder/reinforcement-fine-tuning">Improve decisions using feedback that actually measures the task.</Card>
<Card title="Continued pretraining" href="/ladder/continued-pretraining">Develop domain familiarity where evidence justifies further training.</Card>
<Card title="Custom pretraining" href="/ladder/custom-pretraining">Consider a specialist model architecture and its full cost.</Card>
</Cards>

The site’s broader purpose is to distill model intelligence into enterprise capability. **Model distillation** is also the name of one particular training technique; it is not required for every enterprise agent.

## Keep the comparison honest

Use representative tasks and a simpler baseline. Measure correctness, business usefulness, time, cost, and human review. Keep failures and unchanged results. For retrieval, the [context experiments](/patterns) show why extra complexity must earn its place.

For a complete system, explore [architecture](/architecture). For practical applications, start with [use cases](/use-cases).
