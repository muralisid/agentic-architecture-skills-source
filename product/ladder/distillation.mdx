Everything else on this ladder tries to make an agent better. This one keeps quality roughly the same and makes it cheaper and faster, by having a large model teach a small one. It earns its place twice over: it is the answer to "this costs too much at volume", and it is the published repair for the damage that fine-tuning does. The one thing to remember: a student can only be as good as its teacher, so distil only what the teacher already does well.
</PlainTerms>

## How a model teaches a model

Supervised fine-tuning shows a model the right answer. Distillation shows it something richer: the teacher's full distribution over possible next <Term k="token">tokens</Term>, not just the one it picked. The student learns that "transferred" was chosen, and that "moved" was nearly chosen, and that "flew" was never in contention. Those relative preferences carry far more information per example than a bare answer does, which is why a student can approach teacher quality with surprisingly modest training.

The modern refinement is **on-policy distillation**: rather than the student passively imitating teacher transcripts, the student generates, and the teacher grades the student's own attempts. This matters because it teaches the student in the situations the student actually gets itself into, including its own characteristic mistakes, rather than in the situations the teacher would have been in. The published economics: comparable quality at **9 to 30 times lower training compute** than scaling supervised fine-tuning.

## The second use: repairing forgetting

The previous chapter left a problem open. Fine-tuning on your domain degrades general capability, and adapters do not prevent it.

The published repair is distillation with a specific teacher: **the pre-fine-tune version of the same model.** The fine-tuned student re-learns the general behaviour from the model it used to be, while keeping the domain behaviour it just gained. This works because the two objectives are not actually in conflict; the fine-tune simply drifted weights it did not need to drift, and the original model is a perfect reference for where they belong.

So distillation is maintenance, not only compression. If you run chapter 4 or chapter 7, plan to run this afterwards, and let your general-capability eval tell you whether you need to.

## When it pays: the arithmetic

Distillation is a fixed cost that buys a lower marginal cost. The decision is arithmetic, not fashion:

- **Volume.** The saving is per call, so the fixed cost divides by call count. Divide the fixed cost by the saving on each call. That gives the number of calls it takes to pay the cost back. Compare that with your real call volume. No general threshold applies. The inputs are your own costs and volumes.
- **Narrowness.** A student holds teacher quality on a bounded task, not in general. The narrower the task, the smaller the student can be.
- **A locked eval.** The claim "the student holds quality" is a measurement, and it needs the eval to exist before the student does. The tension worth naming: the business case needs a fixed eval set, and a live learning loop keeps rewriting the eval set. Decide which programme you are running.

And one hard rule: **never distil a capability the teacher does not reliably have.** The student learns the teacher's errors with the same fidelity as its skills, minus the scale that occasionally rescued the teacher.

## In utilities and mining

Suppose a larger model performs a bounded evidence-extraction task adequately. A smaller model may be worth evaluating for routine cases if the volume and cost justify training and maintenance.

Build examples with source references and checks. Hold out unusual documents, missing evidence, and conflicting records. Compare the student with the original model and a simpler extraction approach. Route out-of-scope cases to the existing reviewed process.

Do not assume distillation repairs every regression caused by earlier training. Accept it only when evaluation shows that the required capabilities are retained and the complete operating cost improves.

## The test

There is no climb from here. Distillation follows the other options rather than replacing them: after [adapters](/ladder/adapters-and-fine-tuning) or [continued pretraining](/ladder/continued-pretraining) as the forgetting repair, or after any of them as the volume play. If what remains wrong is judgement on gradeable outcomes, that is [reinforcement fine-tuning](/ladder/reinforcement-fine-tuning).
