The estate you already own has fourteen layers. The agent system you build across it has [seven planes](/architecture). Each page below gives one layer's target-state architecture, the mechanisms that implement it with their exact protocols and measured numbers, the contested choices with verdicts, its cross-cutting concern row, and what the evidence does not support.

| Layer | Target state in one line |

| [R07 Agent platform](/layers/r07-agent-platform) | Buy the runtime, build the harness: explicit termination, artifacts-as-state, separate verification, registry with eval-gated promotion |

## How the pages are built

Each page condenses one research track. The tracks, with their briefs, findings, vendor maps, and dated sources, are in [the research library](/library/layers). Where the evidence does not support a claim, the page says so and names the refusal; the [decision catalog](/decisions) carries every contested choice with its verdict and discriminator.
