## Target state

Identity is the perimeter: every control in this layer hangs off the agent having a first-class identity. Every agent holds a registered, least-privileged, short-lived identity with a named owner and risk tier; shared human credentials do not exist. Presence-grade capability (mailbox, meeting seat, directory visibility) is granted per capability surface to a small set of durable teammates whose Sponsors accept the discoverability and phishing surface. All consequential actions pass a deterministic policy decision point in the tool-call path that the agent cannot bypass. Probabilistic detection feeds the SOC through the SIEM, with agent-specific runbooks and new evidence types. Guardrails run as an advisory layer, never as the boundary. The four zones (access control, money movement, safety actuation, regulatory records) stay deterministic at the decision: models may inform, never decide. This layer decides; the [R03 gateway](/layers/r03-integration-fabric) enforces what its identities and policies decide.

## Mechanisms

### The threat canon

Four documents define the mid-2026 threat model. OWASP's Top 10 for Agentic Applications (released 9 December 2025) runs ASI01 goal hijack through ASI10 rogue agents; the two that reshape enterprise controls are **ASI06 memory poisoning**, which turns agent memory into a persistence mechanism, and **ASI09 human-trust exploitation**, which makes agent-to-human communication an attack surface. MITRE ATLAS added fourteen agent-focused techniques in October 2025 and now stands at 16 tactics, 84 techniques, and 42 case studies. Microsoft's failure-mode taxonomy v2.0 (June 2026) counts **99 MCP-related CVEs in 2025** alone. Six national cyber agencies published joint adoption guidance naming five risk categories (April-May 2026): start low-risk, grant no broad access. The identity baseline the canon lands on: non-human identities outnumber humans roughly **100:1** and **97% are over-privileged** [vendor], while only 23% of organizations have a formal agent identity strategy and 92% doubt legacy IAM can manage the risk (CSA/Oasis, January 2026 [sponsored]).

### Identity mechanics: JIT, RAR, revocation

Register every agent with a named owner and risk tier, and eliminate shared credentials first. Least privilege for agents is **just-in-time elevation over a minimal baseline with automatic drop-back**: standing permissions stay minimal, elevation is granted per task, and it expires on its own (published as the least-privilege pattern for agents, July 2026 [vendor]). Single-action authorization uses **OAuth Rich Authorization Requests (RFC 9396)**, which put the specific action's parameters inside the token instead of granting a scope. Credentials are **short-lived and cryptographically anchored** (the CISA guidance term), and the **NIST NCCoE demonstration project (February 2026) composes OAuth, SPIFFE, and MCP** into exactly this chain. The kill switch is an identity-platform feature, not an afterthought: Okta shipped one at GA (April 2026 [vendor]), and **time-to-revoke an agent credential** is the metric that proves it works; drill it. Evidence that the estate is ready: all four identity majors GA'd agent identity within twelve months (CyberArk November 2025, Okta April 2026, SailPoint May 2026, Entra's agent registry converging into Agent 365 April 2026) [all vendor].

### The policy decision point

The authorization boundary is policy-as-code in the tool-call path: a policy decision point the agent cannot bypass, enforced at the R03 gateway. Engine choice is a property trade: **Cedar evaluates in sub-millisecond time and is formally verified, but is stateless**; **OPA/Rego handles richer joins** against external data; OpenFGA/SpiceDB cover relationship-based authorization. Two deterministic complements shipped in 2025-2026: **Microsoft's FIDES information-flow control**, in Agent Framework v1.3, held AgentDojo to **zero policy-violating injections versus 20-152 without it** [vendor]; **Bedrock Automated Reasoning checks (GA 6 August 2025)** verify outputs against formal logic [vendor]. The PDP fails close: when the engine is down, consequential actions stop.

### Guardrails are advisory

Published measurement settles this. Attack success against major commercial guardrails: roughly **72% (Azure Prompt Shield), 77% (Protect AI v1), 72.5% (NeMo jailbreak detect)** (ACL LLMSEC 2025), rising to **100% in some configurations**. The one configuration measured at **0% bypass achieved it at 16.22% false positives and roughly 1.5 seconds of added latency** (arXiv 2605.06669). Latency alone separates the layers: policy-engine evaluation runs sub-millisecond to low-millisecond against roughly 1.5 seconds per LLM guardrail call, **three orders of magnitude**. Guardrails are also themselves a target: guardrail-triggered denial of service is exploitable (arXiv 2410.02916). The position this evidence forces: guardrails retain value for content policy, PII detection, and telemetry, where misses are tolerable; they never authorize an action.

### The mandate-bound payment stack

The card networks built the deterministic-boundary architecture independently, which is the strongest convergence evidence in this layer. **Visa Trusted Agent Protocol (October 2025)**: issuer-anchored delegation tokens **scoped by amount, merchant, and category**. **Mastercard Agent Pay (April 2025)**: agentic tokens bound to agent, merchant, and consent, **revocable in real time**. **Google AP2 (September 2025, 60+ partners)**: cryptographically signed **W3C Verifiable Credential mandates carrying hard constraints**. All three are deterministic authorization consuming probabilistic signals. The caveat this guide states plainly: **the mandate does not protect intent formation**. AP2 was red-teamed via prompt injection operating within mandate bounds (January 2026); the gate bounds the blast radius, and upstream injection defense plus human confirmation of intent remain necessary.

### The four zones, zone by zone

Decisions in the four zones are made by deterministic rules over verifiable credentials and policies; model outputs are advisory inputs (see [deterministic zones](/architecture/deterministic-zones)). Three zones already run ML signals inside them, and that is the design, not a violation: **Entra ID Protection feeds ML risk scores into deterministic Conditional Access**; **Mastercard Decision Intelligence scores every network transaction while authorization stays rule-bound**; filings are drafted with AI and attested deterministically. **Safety actuation is the one zone where standards exclude ML from the function itself: IEC 61511 requires that the safety-instrumented system not use AI, and ISO/IEC TR 5469:2024 frames AI outside the deterministic safety function.** Records regimes mandate accountability and immutable records, not deterministic generation: FINRA 24-09 (June 2024) confirms technology-neutral obligations continue to apply; FINRA 25-07 (April 2025) asks for comment rather than settling a new interpretation; ESMA said the equivalent in May 2024; Deloitte Australia's partial contract refund (roughly A$440k, October 2025) is the cautionary case.

### The structural finding

Why the boundary must be architectural: **token-uniform processing means a transformer cannot separate commands from data by training alone** (Bhattarai and Vu, February 2026); without architectural mediation, authorization becomes an exploit-discovery problem. **EchoLeak (CVE-2025-32711, CVSS 9.3)** is the incident that instantiates it: zero-click exfiltration through a Copilot-attached mailbox, with the injected instructions indistinguishable from content at the token level. Unit 42 has since documented in-the-wild indirect prompt injection. The design consequence runs through every mechanism above: assume injection sometimes succeeds; identity scoping, the PDP, and mandates exist to bound what a compromised agent can do.

## Design decisions

- **SIEM extension vs dedicated AI-security platforms** ([CD-13](/decisions#cd-13)): the market is resolving toward platform absorption: six AI-security acquisitions in roughly fourteen months (Cisco/Robust Intelligence 2024; Palo Alto/Protect AI July 2025; SentinelOne/Prompt Security ~$250M; F5/CalypsoAI $180M; CrowdStrike/Pangea ~$260M; Check Point/Lakera ~$300M) plus two NHI deals (Cisco/Astrix ~$400M; Cyera/Oasis ~$1B). Buy accordingly, but absorption is not parity: **behavioral baselining of agents remains unsolved in mainstream SIEMs**, and guardian agents persist as a distinct runtime category (Gartner: 10-15% of the agentic market by 2030). Independents differentiate on agent-graph posture and research depth.
- **Standalone guardrail engines vs platform-native plus deterministic gates** ([CD-16](/decisions#cd-16)): deterministic gates own the authorization boundary; guardrails are advisory wherever they run. Choose standalone guardrails for cross-model content policy and PII coverage, platform-native where the platform already hosts the workload, and never let either authorize an action.

## Evidence and limits

The incident record: EchoLeak (CVE-2025-32711, CVSS 9.3), zero-click exfiltration via a Copilot-attached mailbox; 99 MCP-related CVEs counted for 2025 (Microsoft failure-mode taxonomy v2.0, June 2026); in-the-wild indirect prompt injection documented by Unit 42; supply-chain compromise of agent tooling. Vendor-published and carried as such: the NHI ratios (~100:1, 97% over-privileged), the CSA/Oasis survey [sponsored], the identity-platform GA dates and the JIT pattern, the FIDES AgentDojo result, and Bedrock Automated Reasoning checks. Verified only to headline: the RSAC 2026 baselining-gap reporting (the primary report could not be retrieved). Secondary-sourced: the exact publication date of the six-nation guidance; the FINRA 25-07 reading traces through a vendor blog and should be verified against finra.org before it carries weight. One refusal: the widely repeated count of agents registered on Microsoft's platform is an earnings self-report and this page excludes it. "Models may inform, never decide" is the authors' formulation of the zone principle; the payment stack is its convergent evidence, not a standard citing it. Re-verify quarterly: the AI-security acquisition ledger (the fastest-consolidating market in this guide), SIEM behavioral baselining (post-RSAC 2027), the NCCoE project's published output, and the FINRA 25-07 disposition.
