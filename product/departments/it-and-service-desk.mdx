## Monday, 8:15, the desk

The weekend queue is not a wall of tickets; it is a briefing. The triage agent classified everything as it arrived, enriched each ticket with the user's device, entitlements, and history, resolved the routine password and access requests through pre-approved patterns, and left Tier 2 a short list of the genuinely hard ones, each with the diagnostic context already gathered. The knowledge agent answered how-do-I questions all weekend from a corpus one named person owns, refusing politely where the answer was not in it, which is exactly what you want. Your best engineer starts the week on the two problems that deserve them. All of it deployable now.

Here is the whole plan.

## Start with these two use cases

1. **Ticket triage and routine fulfilment.** Classify, enrich, route, and execute the bounded, reversible requests through pre-approved patterns. First because volume is highest, labels are cleanest, and every resolved ticket is a training example your evals can use.
2. **Knowledge answers with refusal.** Answer from a curated, owned corpus; refuse and escalate on weak evidence. Second because answer quality is entirely a curation question, and this teaches your organisation corpus ownership on the cheapest possible stakes.

Access decisions are not on the list because they are not a use case: entitlement is decided by rules in your identity platform, always. The agent gathers context and executes approved patterns; it never decides who gets access to what.

## The plan, step by step

1. **Appoint the knowledge-corpus owner.** One person, review cadence, freshness measured. Done when stale articles have an expiry date.
2. **Wire the agents through your ITSM and identity APIs as the requesting user.** No service-account agents. Done when the audit log shows the human identity on every action.
3. **Ship triage in suggest mode.** The agent proposes classification and routing; dispatchers accept or fix. Done when acceptance is stable above 85%.
4. **Turn on routine fulfilment for five pre-approved patterns.** Group membership, licence assignment, standard software. Done when reopen rate on agent-fulfilled tickets is at or below the human baseline.
5. **Ship knowledge answers with the refusal behaviour visible.** Done when resolution is measured by reopen rate, not closure rate.
6. **Expand patterns and intents by evidence,** and give Tier 2 the time back deliberately: their new job is exceptions and the corpus.

## Worked example: the 40,000-ticket quarter

A mid-size desk runs 40,000 tickets a quarter. The honest published comparator: agent-eligible conversations were under 10% of volume, and the agent completed 35% of those without a human. Build the business case on that shape, roughly 1,400 tickets a quarter genuinely closed by the agent plus triage lift on everything, and the programme survives its first measurement quarter. If a vendor promises 70% deflection, ask which metric, because auto-closure is containment wearing a different name, and reopen rate is where it hides.

## Your agent roster

| Agent | What it does | Autonomy |
|---|---|---|
| Triage agent | Classifies, enriches, routes | Executes routing; suggests on ambiguity |
| Provisioning agent | Executes pre-approved fulfilment patterns | Executes; identity rules decide entitlement |
| Knowledge agent | Answers from the owned corpus, refuses on weak evidence | Resolves; escalates |
| Diagnostic agent | Correlates telemetry, proposes fixes with evidence | Proposes; human applies |

## What stays human

The long tail, which is where the tickets that hurt live. Major incidents. Every entitlement decision. And corpus ownership, which is the highest-leverage human job this playbook creates.

## The economics

Cost per resolved ticket including Tier 2 minutes, reopen rate beside resolution always, 90th-percentile time to resolution, and corpus freshness against policy change. Cheap workload, honest numbers, fast feedback: that is why this department goes first in most enterprises, and why it earns the platform the rest of the company inherits.

## Watchouts

- Auto-closure inflates resolution; measure reopen rate or measure nothing.
- IT policy changes weekly and corpora rot fastest here; an unowned corpus is worse than none.
- No published head-to-head exists between embedded ITSM-vendor agents and external agents on identical ticket sets; run your own bake-off on your own tickets.
