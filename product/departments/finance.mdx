## Day three of close, 8:00

The close calendar looks like it did last quarter, but the tail is quiet. Overnight, the exception agent investigated every failed match: evidence gathered, supplier history attached, disposition proposed, each waiting for an accountant's one-click decision. The reconciliation agent explains variances instead of listing them, with each explanation citing the ledger lines behind it. Commentary drafts for the management pack are ready against actuals, and the controller edits instead of assembling. No payment moved without a mandate. Nothing was attested by anything that cannot be held accountable. All of it deployable now.

Here is the whole plan.

## Start with these two use cases

1. **Invoice exception investigation.** The agent works each match failure to a proposed disposition with the evidence attached; the accountant decides. First because it is the highest-volume, best-labelled work in the department.
2. **Close assistance.** Assemble schedules, chase open items, draft commentary against actuals. Second because the value concentrates in the compressed tail of close, where the overtime lives.

Deliberately absent: anything that authorizes a payment or attests a filing. Movement of money and formal records are deterministic zones. Agents prepare; mandates and accountable humans decide.

## The plan, step by step

1. **Sign the deterministic-zone page with your CFO**: payments and attestations are decided by rules and people, never by model output. Done when it is signed.
2. **Route numbers through the semantic layer,** never through the model. A numeric answer either traces to governed figures or it is refused. Done when high-stakes numeric questions produce citations or refusals, nothing else.
3. **Ship exception investigation on one entity.** Done when exceptions cleared per accountant-day rises with rework flat.
4. **Preserve segregation of duties across agents**: the agent that prepares a payment proposal is never the agent that checks it, and neither approves. Done when the control matrix includes agents and internal audit has signed it.
5. **Ship close assistance for one close.** Done when the tail's overtime hours fall measurably.
6. **Add the reporting draft agent last.** Drafting is allowed; attestation is not delegable. Done when reviewers edit drafts instead of writing from blank pages.

## Worked example: the three-way match that failed

An invoice fails its match at 30 units against a PO of 28. The agent pulls the goods receipt, finds the over-delivery documented and signed at the dock, checks the supplier's tolerance terms, sees 30 is inside the agreed 10%, and proposes approval with each evidence item linked. The accountant approves in one click; the trail records who decided with what in front of them. The same flow with a contested receipt proposes rejection with a drafted supplier query instead. Either way, the human decided, the mandate rules governed, and no payment moved on a model's opinion.

## Your agent roster

| Agent | What it does | Autonomy |
|---|---|---|
| Exception agent | Investigates match failures, proposes dispositions | Proposes; accountant decides |
| Reconciliation agent | Explains variances with cited evidence | Explains only |
| Close assistant | Assembles, chases, drafts commentary | Executes routine; drafts for review |
| Reporting draft agent | Drafts narrative against governed numbers | Drafts; attestation human |

## What stays human

Judgment, materiality, attestation, and fiduciary accountability. Every payment mandate. And skepticism about numeric analysis: in observed-task testing, spreadsheet analysis with an assistant was slower and less accurate than without one, so any proposal centred on ad-hoc numeric analysis deserves your doubt.

## The economics

Exceptions cleared per accountant-day with rework flat, close-tail overtime hours, refusal rate on numeric questions (which should be visible and stable, because refusals are the semantic layer doing its job), and segregation-of-duties violations at zero.

## Watchouts

- No published production case exists of autonomous payment authority in a regulated enterprise, and you do not want to be the case study.
- Loose human entitlements become loose agent entitlements: an agent acting as the requesting user inherits your permission sprawl. Clean it first.
- The numeric-analysis weakness is documented; route numbers through governed semantics, not prompts.
