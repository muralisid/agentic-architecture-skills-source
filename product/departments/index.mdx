Each playbook: the vision of the function with agents on the team, two starter use cases chosen for you, the plan with done-whens, one worked example, the roster, and the watchouts.

- **[IT and service desk](/departments/it-and-service-desk)**: where production agents actually concentrate, and where your platform gets built on the cheapest honest stakes.
- **[Customer service](/departments/customer-service)**: agent-assist first, resolution over containment always, and how not to join the rollback statistics.
- **[Finance](/departments/finance)**: exception investigation and close assistance, with money movement and attestation deterministic forever.
- **[Human resources](/departments/hr)**: service queries and onboarding, while employment decisions stay human and consulted.
- **[Sales](/departments/sales)**: research and hygiene reps actually use, inbound worked at midnight, commitments never delegated.
- **[Marketing](/departments/marketing)**: campaign mechanics and grounded drafting, throttled to the review capacity that is your real constraint.
- **[Supply chain](/departments/supply-chain)**: exceptions dispositioned inside thresholds with compensation armed, trade-offs left to planners.
