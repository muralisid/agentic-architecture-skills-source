This is the reading behind the research pages: the papers that proposed the ideas under test, the ones that supplied the benchmarks and metrics, and the ones the recommendation leans on. Each row says what the paper showed and whether the experiments here agree with it. Vendor-published results are marked as such. ## Several vectors per document, and the unit of indexing

| Paper | What it shows | How the results here relate |
|---|---|---|
| Weller, Boratko, Naim, Lee, On the Theoretical Limitations of Embedding-Based Retrieval, arXiv:2508.21038, Aug 2025. https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.21038 | For a fixed embedding dimension, some top-k result sets are unreachable by any single-vector index; state-of-the-art embedders fail the LIMIT dataset built to show it | Confirmed: pooled dense 0.314 on LIMIT-small; per-attribute cards 0.988, fine blind windows 0.924 |
| Khattab and Zaharia, ColBERT, SIGIR 2020. https://arxiv.org/abs/2004.12832 ; Santhanam et al., ColBERTv2, NAACL 2022. https://arxiv.org/abs/2112.01488 | Token-level multi-vector retrieval (late interaction); storage 10 to 30 times dense retrieval is the standing critique | Bounds the design: coarse purpose views are the compromise between one vector and per-token vectors; storage is a property of the corpus (44 cards per document on LIMIT, 2.8 on SciFact) |
| Zhang et al., Multi-View Document Representation Learning, ACL 2022. https://aclanthology.org/2022.acl-long.414/ | Several learned viewer embeddings per document for different query views | The closest academic phrase to the idea; the views there are learned and fixed, not conditioned on an objective |
| Kong et al., Multi-Aspect Dense Retrieval, KDD 2022 | Explicit aspect embeddings from product metadata | Same family: fixed aspects, set by available metadata |
| Dong et al., Multi-view Content-aware Indexing, EMNLP Findings 2024. https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.15103 | Each section indexed under three fixed views (raw text, keywords, summary) | A fixed-view multi-representation index; consistent with corpus-derived views being the durable positive |
| Chen et al., Dense X Retrieval, EMNLP 2024. https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.06648 | Proposition-level indexing: +10.1 recall at 20 unsupervised | Granularity matters and is workload-dependent, which is what the SciFact boundary shows |
| Duarte et al., LumberChunker, EMNLP Findings 2024. https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.17526 ; Zhong et al., Mix-of-Granularity, 2024. https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.00456 | Model-placed chunk boundaries; routing among granularities | Both decide a granularity globally and require reading every item; the recommendation keeps the model off the per-item path |
| Anthropic, Contextual Retrieval, Sep 2024 (vendor). https://www.anthropic.com/news/contextual-retrieval | Prepending situating context to each chunk before embedding; about $1.02 per million document tokens with caching | The published floor for touching every chunk once with a model; the two-pass design avoids that cost by construction |
| Weller et al., When do Generative Query and Document Expansions Fail?, EACL 2024. https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.08541 | Expansions help weak retrievers and can hurt strong ones | Consistent with purpose cards hurting on SciFact under a capable hybrid |

## Conditioning the index on an instruction or objective

| Paper | What it shows | How the results here relate |
|---|---|---|
| Su et al., INSTRUCTOR, ACL Findings 2023. https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.09741 | One encoder that produces task-specific embeddings from an instruction | The execution layer that subsumes objective-conditioned views |
| Weller et al., FollowIR, EMNLP 2024. https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.15246 | A benchmark with two sets of human judgements per query, before and after the instruction changes, and the p-MRR score | Used as the real-data test in round 5; card re-selection scored p-MRR about zero |
| Weller et al., Promptriever, ICLR 2025. https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.11136 | Retrievers trained to follow instructions like language models | The strong baseline any objective-conditioning proposal must beat; deliberately deferred here because it would widen the negative |
| Feng et al., GSTransform, 2025. https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.24754 | Per-instruction embedding views without re-encoding the corpus | Delivers the embed-once economics the round-5 architecture aimed at, without the view-design step |
| Qidwai et al., CAMI, 2026. https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.28365 | An agent proposes candidate enrichment indexes and selects a portfolio under a cost budget | Conditions index construction on the corpus and a budget, not on the objective; consistent with the negative here |
| Microsoft Research, GraphRAG auto-tuning, 2024 (vendor). https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/patterns/blog/graphrag-auto-tuning-provides-rapid-adaptation-to-new-domains/ | Extraction schema generated from roughly a one percent corpus sample | Corpus-derived design, the form that worked here |

## The model designs; classical machinery executes

| Paper | What it shows | How the results here relate |
|---|---|---|
| Wan et al., TnT-LLM, KDD 2024. https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.12173 | A model builds a taxonomy from corpus samples and pseudo-labels; embedding plus logistic regression reaches 0.658 accuracy against 0.655 for the frontier model used directly | The economics plank of the recommendation, and the closest published design to the corpus-sample step that worked |
| Pham et al., TopicGPT, NAACL 2024. https://aclanthology.org/2024.naacl-long.164/ | Model-generated topics align better with human categories than classical topic models | Supports model naming and merging of discovered topics |
| Hsieh et al., Distilling Step-by-Step, ACL Findings 2023. https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.02301 ; SetFit, 2022. https://github.com/huggingface/setfit | Small models taught by large ones can beat the teacher at execution; few-shot small models beat GPT-3 at 1,600 times smaller | Why the gate and the classifiers should be small models, not frontier calls |
| Databricks, Scaling document classification to 100k+ labels, Jul 2026 (vendor). https://www.databricks.com/blog/scaling-document-classification-100k-labels | Vector shortlist plus small model at about one hundredth the per-document cost of direct frontier classification, at higher accuracy (0.81 against 0.76) | A production-scale parallel to the two-pass constant factor |
| Arora et al., EVAPORATE, VLDB 2023. https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.09433 | A model synthesises extraction functions from a sample that then run over the whole corpus, cutting token cost by two orders of magnitude | The same shape of saving, measured independently |
| Sarthi et al., RAPTOR, ICLR 2024. https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.18059 | Cluster embeddings, summarise clusters recursively, index all levels | The closest cluster-then-summarise system; uses a Gaussian mixture where this programme used density clustering |
| Grootendorst, BERTopic, 2022. https://maartengr.github.io/BERTopic/ | Density clustering over reduced embeddings with outlier reduction, topic merging, and guided or zero-shot topic lists | The established machinery used in rounds 2 to 5, as the maintainer required, rather than a home-grown variant |
| Edge et al., GraphRAG, 2024 (Microsoft). https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.16130 ; TREX, Mar 2025. https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.02922 ; LightRAG, EMNLP 2025. https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.05779 | Model-intensive graph indexing for global sensemaking; indexing bills of $51 to $389 per benchmark corpus; lightweight alternatives | The cost of letting a model read everything, and the space a topics-plus-clusters vector design competes in; see the guide's [knowledge-graph study](/library/techniques/when-a-knowledge-graph-earns-its-keep) |

## Diversity, noise, and distractors

| Paper | What it shows | How the results here relate |
|---|---|---|
| Carbonell and Goldstein, MMR, SIGIR 1998. https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/290941.291025 | The standard relevance-diversity re-ranking; ships in most vector stores | The harvest bought the same coverage as MMR at lambda 0.3 for half the relevance cost |
| Diverse Multi-Answer Retrieval with DPPs, COLING 2022. https://aclanthology.org/2022.coling-1.194/ ; SMART-RAG, 2024. https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.13992 | Determinantal point processes for diverse context selection | The DPP reached the most coverage and admitted the most distractors (0.328) |
| DIVA, NAACL 2025. https://aclanthology.org/2025.naacl-long.56/ ; ARAGOG, 2024. https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.01037 | Diversified retrieval wins on ambiguous, multi-answer questions; MMR adds nothing on standard single-answer QA | Scopes where diversification belongs: coverage-driven workloads only |
| Sub-Question Coverage, Salesforce and Georgia Tech, Oct 2024. https://arxiv.org/html/2410.15531v1 ; Beyond Relevance: Information Coverage, ICTIR 2026. https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.08819 | Coverage predicts answer completeness; coverage metrics correlate with nugget coverage | Why S-recall and alpha-nDCG, not recall, are the measurement |
| Cuconasu et al., The Power of Noise, SIGIR 2024. https://arxiv.org/pdf/2401.14887 ; The Powerless Noise, SIGIR 2026. https://arxiv.org/pdf/2607.03615 | Random documents appeared to help generation; the effect was walked back | A caution against deliberate lateral inclusion without measurement |
| Amiraz et al., The Distracting Effect, ACL 2025. https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.06914 | Topically adjacent answerless passages are the most damaging additions | Why the pool must be gated first and the distractor rate measured; the harvest's 0.236 against the DPP's 0.328 |
| Clarke et al., alpha-nDCG, SIGIR 2008. https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/1390334.1390446 | The coverage-aware ranking metric | Used throughout the diversity experiment |

Every arXiv entry in the benchmark repository's bibliography was checked programmatically against its abstract page on 2026-08-20 (title, first author, submission date). Vendor-published results are labelled; conference venues are as published and are re-checked before any submission.
