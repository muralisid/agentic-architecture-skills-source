- **[Manufacturing and supply chain](/industries/manufacturing-and-supply-chain)**: exceptions worked overnight, the OT boundary absolute, and honest numbers about lights-out.
- **[Public sector](/industries/public-sector)**: cases arriving decision-ready, every decision explainable on appeal, eligibility never delegated.
