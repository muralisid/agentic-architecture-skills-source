## Wednesday, 9:10, the casework team

The team lead's stand-up starts with the queue, and the queue is different now. Every case that arrived overnight is already assembled: documents parsed, completeness checked, missing evidence flagged with a drafted request to the resident, the applicable rules retrieved and cited. The caseworkers open files that are ready to be decided, not ready to be sorted. The enquiry agent answered several hundred routine status and process questions overnight in plain language and three community languages, and escalated the ones that needed a person, each with its history attached. One officer reviews the retention agent's weekly disclosure-preparation run. Nobody's eligibility was decided by a machine; every decision in this building is made by someone who can be named in an appeal. All of it deployable now.

Here is the whole plan to get there.

## Start with these three use cases

1. **Case preparation.** Assemble the file, check completeness, flag missing evidence, draft the summary; the officer decides. First because backlog is your visible failure, preparation is where the hours go, and the decision authority never moves.
2. **Resident enquiries on process and status.** Answer from a governed corpus, escalate with history when it matters. Second because volume is high, the answers are knowable, and plain-language plus translation delivery is a service improvement residents feel immediately.
3. **Records and retention.** Classify, apply statutory schedules, prepare disclosure responses. Third because it is deterministic-friendly work your team never has enough hands for, and it hardens the evidence plane every appeal depends on.

Deliberately absent: eligibility decisions at any autonomy level. Access to essential public services is high-risk under the EU AI Act, every decision here is appealable by design, and an officer who rubber-stamps a ranked list has not decided anything.

## The plan, step by step

1. **Write the decision boundary and publish it internally**: agents prepare and explain; statute, policy, and accountable officials decide. Done when the one-pager is signed and every project charter cites it.
2. **Pick the sovereignty tier before the platform.** Public procurement increasingly names sovereignty frameworks; decide yours first so the shortlist is real. Done when the tier is recorded and procurement agrees.
3. **Appoint corpus owners** for rules, procedures, and letters. Done when each has a named owner and a review cadence.
4. **Ship case preparation for one case type.** Officer override rate is the health signal; near zero means rubber-stamping. Done when cycle time falls and override rate is measured and materially above zero.
5. **Ship the enquiry agent on status and process only.** Disclosure on, escalation staffed, accessibility duties met, resolution measured. Done when resolution holds and complaints do not rise.
6. **Ship retention classification with sampled human review.** Done when disclosure-preparation time falls with sampling quality stable.
7. **Extend case types one at a time,** each with its own evidence pack, because each will one day be examined in an appeal.

## Worked example: the incomplete application

An application arrives with two documents missing and one ambiguous. Within the hour, the preparation agent has parsed what came in, matched it against the checklist for this case type, drafted a plain-language request naming exactly what is missing and why it is needed, and queued the draft for the officer. The officer approves the request in one click. When the documents arrive, the file re-assembles itself and enters the decision queue complete, with a summary, the applicable rules cited, and every piece of evidence paginated for the file. The officer decides. If the resident appeals, the file already shows what was seen, what was requested, and who decided, because the evidence plane was built for appeal from day one.

## Your agent roster

| Agent | What it does | Autonomy |
|---|---|---|
| Case preparation agent | Assembles, checks, flags, summarizes | Prepares; officer decides |
| Enquiry agent | Answers process and status questions from a governed corpus | Resolves routine; escalates with history |
| Records and retention agent | Classifies, applies schedules, prepares disclosure | Executes with sampled review |
| Translation and plain-language agent | Adapts official communication for language and reading level | Drafts; owner approves templates |

## Buy this, build that

Buy within your procurement framework and your sovereignty tier; that decision precedes brand names. Take the productivity assistants your incumbent bundles. Build the rules-and-procedures corpus in-house with named owners, because it is your statute book operationalized, and no vendor is accountable to your ombudsman.

## What stays human

Every exercise of public authority: eligibility, enforcement, discretion, appeal. Empathy at the counter for the resident having the worst week of their year. And the duty that shapes the whole architecture: every decision must be explainable to the person it affects, with the evidence the decision-maker saw available on appeal.

## The economics

Count cycle time to the resident at the 90th percentile, backlog trend, appeal rate and appeal-upheld rate, and enquiry resolution with repeat contact. The metric that matters politically is time to a lawful decision. Containment metrics are actively harmful here: your residents cannot take their business elsewhere, so a deflected contact is a failed contact wearing a good number.

## Watchouts

- No published production case exists of autonomous eligibility decisions, and this page recommends against building one at any autonomy level.
- Merely formal human participation is not sufficient, and regulators say so directly. Show officers evidence, not scores, so a different answer is genuinely reachable.
- Statutory retention is long, and agent memory, traces, and derived artifacts inherit it. Most agent platforms were not designed for a 25-year retention schedule; ask before you buy.
- Public bodies often sit at low data readiness. If a corpus has no owner, the agent on top of it will be confidently wrong in public.
