These examples identify documented building blocks and vendor-reported applications. They do not establish the business results of the proposed agent designs in this guide. Sources were checked on 17 September 2026.

## Video investigation

NVIDIA’s [video-search and summarization blueprint](https://build.nvidia.com/nvidia/video-search-and-summarization/blueprintcard) documents video analysis, search, summarization, and question answering. This supports considering a visual evidence service in a larger investigation. Identity, work-order permissions, coaching policy, and verified outcomes remain separate enterprise responsibilities. Vendor documentation.

## Visual workflows

[Roboflow Workflows](https://docs.roboflow.com/workflows) describes configurable computer-vision processing for images and video. A fixed detection and alert pipeline may be sufficient for a known event. An adaptive agent should be added only when choosing the next evidence-gathering step improves the result. Vendor documentation.

## Document evidence

LandingAI describes [visual grounding for document extraction](https://landing.ai/llms/visual-grounding-and-auditability-how-landingai-ade-makes-every-extraction-defensible), including locating extracted information on the source page. That can help a reviewer inspect an extracted field. It does not establish that a document is authentic, current, or sufficient to satisfy a standard. Vendor documentation.

## Vegetation planning

AiDASH reports [satellite-assisted vegetation management with National Grid](https://www.aidash.com/resource/aidash-transforming-vegetation-management-for-national-grid-using-satellite-analytics-and-ai/). Treat the account as a vendor case study. It is relevant to inspection prioritization, but any extension to adaptive investigation or autonomous work requests needs separate evaluation.

## What to compare before adopting

Check the task, permitted input data, required resolution and freshness, edge or cloud constraints, interfaces, total cost, and human review. Ask whether the system detects, extracts, follows a fixed workflow, or chooses its next action. Those are different capabilities.
