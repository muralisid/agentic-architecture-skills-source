## Where things stand

Each new design example identifies its scope and limitations. A proposed agent extension is not labeled as an operational deployment. Existing experiment results retain the circumstances in which they were obtained; they are not universal performance promises.

The [reference library](/library) contains the deeper explanations. The [architecture](/architecture) and [use cases](/use-cases) show where the evidence affects a decision.
