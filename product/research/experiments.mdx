## Use a result carefully

Look for the dataset, questions, baseline, evaluation measure, model configuration, and uncertainty. Do not infer an industrial deployment result from a text-retrieval benchmark. New modality and agent-loop experiments need their own evidence.

See [how to test a context design](/patterns/how-to-test-a-context-design), [what changed](/research/what-changed), and [proposed investigations](/research/questions).
