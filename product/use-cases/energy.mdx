Consider an energy operator investigating an unexpected change in asset performance. A dashboard shows the change, but the explanation may require weather, operating history, inspections, and maintenance records. The proposed agent helps assemble and test that explanation before an engineer decides what to do.

## Plan the work

A proposed planning assistant assembles demand assumptions, weather scenarios, project constraints, and source dates into a reviewable brief. Forecasting and optimization tools produce their own estimates; the agent explains inputs and differences. It does not replace an approved engineering or investment decision.

## Produce and investigate performance

Start with a specific variance against an agreed operating baseline. Ask what changed, which measurements are trustworthy, and which additional check could distinguish an equipment issue from expected operating conditions.

## Maintain the assets

Use inspection and maintenance evidence to prepare a work package. A planning team checks access, spares, staffing, outage dependencies, and the engineering recommendation. Reuse the [safety-coaching example](/use-cases/safety-coaching) for workforce learning, with approved site procedures and reviewed incident evidence.

## Deliver value and demonstrate performance

An agent could prepare evidence for contractual performance reviews or investigate discrepancies between accepted meter records and reports. Keep billing changes and commercial commitments under the responsible owner's authority. Shared patterns include [compliance evidence](/use-cases/compliance-evidence), [recurring audit issues](/use-cases/systemic-audit-issues), and [commercial proposals](/use-cases/commercial-growth).

## Separate potential value from proof

Measure accepted findings, false alerts, investigation time, review cost, and useful work packages. Measure avoided loss only against a defensible comparison. The IEA's [Energy and AI report, 2025](https://www.iea.org/reports/energy-and-ai/ai-for-energy-optimisation-and-innovation) surveys AI applications across energy and notes the difficulty of generalizing benefits beyond individual cases. It does not validate the agent workflows proposed here. Reviewed 18 September 2026.
