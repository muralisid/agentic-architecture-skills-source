At a mine, trucks carry ore from the pit to the processing plant. The fleet system counts the truck tonnes. Belt scales at the plant weigh the ore again. When the two disagree by more than the agreed tolerance, someone must find out why. The official figure feeds royalties, tax and market reports, so the gap matters. Today a mine engineer or a metallurgist chases the cause by email and spreadsheet. In this design an agent checks timing, instruments and material, and prepares a reconciliation case. A named owner accepts any adjustment. The agent cannot write the accepted production record.

## How it runs today

The trigger is the daily production report, or the month-end reconciliation, showing a gap larger than the tolerance. The work then runs in eleven steps.

| Step | Who | System | How the data moves |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Count loads | Fleet system, dispatcher | Fleet management (Modular DISPATCH, Cat MineStar, Wenco) | Every truck load is logged: truck, shovel, material, destination, payload. The dispatcher fixes wrong tags by hand |
| 2. Weigh at the plant | Plant control system | Belt scales to the historian | Tonnes per hour. Shift totals stored in the historian |
| 3. Assay | Lab | LIMS | Grade and moisture from samples |
| 4. Estimate by camera or survey | Vision system, surveyor | Camera volume model. Drone or laser survey | A volume estimate with its calibration. Monthly stockpile surveys arrive as files |
| 5. Daily production report | Production clerk or mine engineer | Excel, fed by fleet and historian exports | Built each morning. Emailed to site leaders before the morning meeting |
| 6. Spot the gap | Mine engineer, metallurgist | Excel, dashboard | Mine tonnes and plant tonnes differ by more than the tolerance |
| 7. Chase the cause | Mine technical services, metallurgist, instrument technician | EAM calibration records. Historian scale faults. Fleet payload settings. LIMS moisture | Emails and phone calls. Each person checks their own system |
| 8. Propose an adjustment | Metallurgist or production accountant | Metal accounting system, or Excel | An adjustment with a comment |
| 9. Approve | Processing or mine manager | Same | Sign-off by email or in the system |
| 10. Month end | Survey, geology, planning, metallurgy | Metal accounting. Mine planning | Stockpile survey. Mass balance. Mined tonnes and grade checked against the block model |
| 11. Formal reports | Finance, corporate reporting, competent person | ERP inventory. Royalty return. Stock exchange report. Annual reserve statement | Signed by named people |

### The oil and gas twin

Oil and gas has the same problem in another form. Well tests, often monthly, estimate each well's flow rate (typical, unsourced). A production data system (Quorum Energy Components, SLB Avocet) builds the daily report. The production accountant then shares the fiscal export volume back to wells and partners. That is called allocation. API's measurement standard, MPMS Chapter 20, sets the method. When the allocation factor leaves its band, a production engineer and a metering technician chase the cause. They check flow computer logs, calibration records and well test validity, by email and spreadsheet. The same agent design applies. The agent reads the allocation system and the calibration records. The accountant re-runs the allocation and signs.

### Handoffs

The case passes between dispatch, mine technical services, plant metallurgy and the lab. Maintenance comes in through the instrument technicians. Finance needs the figure for inventory value and royalties. Corporate reporting needs it for the market.

### Where reporting comes in

The daily production report starts the work. Monthly reconciliation closes it. The figures then flow into quarterly stock exchange reports, royalty returns and the annual reserve statement. In oil and gas they flow into partner statements and regulator returns.

### Cycle time and volumes

- The daily report is built every morning (typical, unsourced).
- The monthly reconciliation closes in the first one to two weeks of the month (typical, unsourced).
- A large open pit logs thousands of truck loads a day (typical, unsourced).

### Pain points

- Sites rely heavily on spreadsheets with weak governance (AusIMM, 23 April 2026).
- Many sites spread the error across the inputs but give the final product no error. The reconciliation then becomes a goal seek that can hide losses (AusIMM, 23 April 2026).
- Belt scales drift. Calibration falls overdue.
- Shift cut-off times differ between systems.
- Moisture and density assumptions turn volume into the wrong tonnes.
- No one owns the gap. Adjustments lose their reasons.

## How agents run it

The agent runs the checks. It reads copies of the fleet database and the historian, never the live systems. It writes a reconciliation case and draft requests. It has no write path to the metal accounting system.

| Step | Agent or person | Reads from, and how | Writes to | Who approves, and where |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Detect | Agent | The daily report data, as an event when the report publishes. Fleet database copy. Historian belt-scale totals. Camera estimates with their uncertainty | Case store | No one |
| 2. Check timing | Agent | Shift boundaries, record timestamps, train load-out times | Case store | No one |
| 3. Check the instruments | Agent | Historian: scale faults, zero drift, downtime. EAM: calibration due and done. Camera health and calibration date | Case store | No one |
| 4. Check the material | Agent | LIMS moisture. Block model density. Material tags in the fleet system | Case store | No one |
| 5. Run the reconciliation | The metal accounting tool calculates | Statistical mass balance, in the style of the AMIRA P754 code | Case store | No one |
| 6. Ask for a measurement | Agent drafts | Gaps in the case | A draft EAM notification, "measurement check", for a belt scale. A survey request for a stockpile | The instrument supervisor or surveyor accepts it |
| 7. Prepare the case | Agent | All of the above | A reconciliation case: size, timing, ranked explanations, evidence, uncertainty. "Unresolved" when evidence is missing | No one. It is advice only |
| 8. Accept an adjustment | Metallurgist or production accountant | The case | The metal accounting system, with the case ID | The named owner. A manager above a set size |
| 9. Formal reports | Finance, competent person | The accepted figures | Royalty return, stock exchange report, reserve statement | Named signatories |

A gap only during a camera outage needs a different check from a gap across every shift. The size and timing of the gap choose the next question. Volume becomes mass only with a stated density. If the evidence is missing or uncalibrated, the case stays unresolved.

### What stays with a person

- Accepting the production figure. It feeds royalties, tax and market disclosure. That record sits in the [formal records zone](/architecture/deterministic-zones#formal-regulatory-records).
- Every adjustment. It changes a record of value.
- The calibration work itself. It needs a permit, and often an isolation.
- Changing the tolerance. That is a policy choice.

### Where the evidence is kept

The case holds every source reference. The adjustment in the metal accounting system carries the case ID and the approver. The camera estimate keeps its original image and calibration. The agent cannot change any of them. That is the [observability and audit trail](/architecture/system-view) capability at work.

### What changes in the systems

- A new "reconciliation case" record.
- A case ID field on adjustments in the metal accounting system.
- An event when the daily report publishes.
- Read access to a copy of the fleet database.
- An EAM notification type, "measurement check".

### What does not change

The calibration procedure stays. So do the fleet system and the metal accounting method. Fiscal metering in oil and gas does not change. The same people sign.

## Who does this today

No named deployment of an agent in metal accounting or hydrocarbon allocation was found by 15 October 2026.

- **The baseline to beat.** Statistical reconciliation under the AMIRA P754 code of practice. ION Algosys and Caspeo sell it [vendor]. A 2023 study tested it at Samarco in Brazil. It is not an agent.
- **Groundhog Apps** sells a "Reconciliation Agent" that flags grade and volume variance [vendor]. The product page is undated.
- **TCS** published an undated white paper on agents for hydrocarbon accounting [vendor].
- **FlowShare** is a hackathon prototype with "auditor" and "accountant" agents for joint venture allocation. Its accuracy figure is self-reported.

AusIMM summarised expert views on 23 April 2026. AI depends on clean, governed data. Experts must check its outputs. Applying AI to control systems is risky.

## Where this sits

The [four questions](/architecture/agent-types) place this agent as follows. Its role is assist. Its permissions are read only on the fleet database copy, historian totals, LIMS and EAM calibration records. It writes a reconciliation case and draft measurement requests. It cannot write the metal accounting system. It runs at delegated tasks (A2) on the [autonomy contract](/architecture/autonomy-contract), as designed and as permitted. The owner reviews every case. For the adjustment itself, the agent only assists (A1). A person makes the change. Its impact sits in the figure it informs. That figure is a formal record, and the agent stays outside it.

On the [risk profile](/architecture/risk-profile) it is Tier 2 (enhanced). Production systems of record and confidential figures score Medium. It is Tier 3 (strict) if your classification treats scale and sensor feeds as control-system data. It starts at curated learning (L1). People choose the reference measurements it learns from.

## Measure the result

Measure the error against an accepted reference and the share of gaps left unresolved. Measure investigation time and the cost of calibration and review. Compare against statistical reconciliation alone. Track the money effect only after the measurement proves reliable.

## Start with a trial

Take three months of daily reports with gaps the metallurgist has already explained. Run the agent on them, with no live requests. Check its ranked explanations against what was found. Then go live at one plant, with drafts only. Register the agent through the [agent lifecycle](/architecture/agent-lifecycle), with a named sponsor.

## Evidence and limits

No named deployment of this agent exists. The as-is is typical practice. The cycle times and volumes are typical and unsourced.

AusIMM (23 April 2026) is the only dated source on current practice. It reports heavy use of spreadsheets and error spread that can hide losses. Statistical reconciliation is the proven method. This design adds the evidence gathering around it. That addition has not been tested.

The camera measurement comes from the author's own work. The agent extension is a proposal.
