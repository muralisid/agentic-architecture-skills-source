Choose a problem that matters to the business. Each example explains the current work, the proposed agent contribution, the evidence it needs, and the people responsible for the result.

## What would you like to improve?

<Cards>
<Card title="Safety coaching from CCTV events" href="/use-cases/safety-coaching">Help people learn from risky situations while keeping incident evidence and teaching material distinct.</Card>
<Card title="Investigate production discrepancies" href="/use-cases/production-measurement">Combine visual measurements with production records to find and explain differences.</Card>
<Card title="Prioritize vegetation inspections" href="/use-cases/vegetation-inspection">Turn image-based risk candidates into evidence-backed field work.</Card>
<Card title="Collect and check compliance evidence" href="/use-cases/compliance-evidence">Connect standards to operational checks, responsible teams, and inspectable evidence.</Card>
<Card title="Investigate recurring audit issues" href="/use-cases/systemic-audit-issues">Use patterns across sites to ask better questions and test possible systemic causes.</Card>
<Card title="Turn delivery knowledge into new opportunities" href="/use-cases/commercial-growth">Support opportunity development with relevant experience, evidence, and accountable commitments.</Card>
<Card title="Investigate a water-network anomaly" href="/use-cases/water-operations">Bring telemetry, asset history, and field evidence into one bounded investigation.</Card>
</Cards>
