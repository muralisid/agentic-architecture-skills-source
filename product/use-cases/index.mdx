## How this map is organized

Established frameworks help describe the work before choosing AI. APQC publishes industry process frameworks for [healthcare providers](https://www.apqc.org/resource-library/resource-listing/apqc-process-classification-framework-pcf-healthcare-provider-pdf) and [utilities](https://www.apqc.org/resource-library/resource-listing/apqc-process-classification-framework-pcf-utilities-pdf-version). WHO's [digital-health classification](https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/9789240081949) organizes uses of digital technology around health needs. EPA's [Effective Utility Management guidance](https://www.epa.gov/sustainable-water-infrastructure/things-local-officials-should-know-about-effective-water) helps utilities examine outcomes across their service. Sources reviewed 18 September 2026.

The visual maps here simplify the journey for readers. They are original teaching maps informed by those references, not reproductions of a standard or certification of an agent design. A working process still needs named owners, local requirements, and an evaluation.
