## How the guidance is maintained

Existing material is a starting point for review, not an unquestionable authority. Recommendations change when stronger evidence or a simpler approach warrants it. External citations identify the underlying source. Original experiments are attributed to the author, and proposed applications are labeled as proposals.

The examples do not imply that every agent extension has been deployed. Product claims and vendor-reported outcomes are distinguished from independent evidence and local measurements.
