Agentic Architecture Skills is a practical guide to having AI agents do company work safely and usefully. It connects business opportunities with architecture, useful knowledge, operating practices and evidence. It rests on three ideas: [the layers you own](/layers), [the lifecycle every agent runs through](/architecture/agent-lifecycle) and [the capabilities you add](/architecture/system-view).

## About the author

## How the guidance is maintained

Existing material is a starting point for review, not an unquestionable authority. Recommendations change when stronger evidence or a simpler approach warrants it. External citations identify the underlying source. Original experiments are attributed to the author, and proposed applications are labelled as proposals.

The examples do not imply that every proposed agent has been deployed. Product claims and vendor-reported outcomes are kept apart from independent evidence and local measurements.
