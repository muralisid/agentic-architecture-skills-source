# R11: Governance, risk & sovereignty

Status: complete (published 2026-08-19). Scope finalized at Batch B kickoff with maintainer POV; see findings.md and sources.md.

## Scope inventory (v0)

- **Regulatory map**: EU AI Act obligations and timeline, NIST AI RMF, DORA, NIS2, sector regimes per vertical (HIPAA, FedRAMP, NERC CIP, RBI, DPDPA), cross-border transfer mechanisms
- **Operating governance**: AI council and use-case intake, risk tiering, impact assessments (DPIA/AIA), model risk management, third-party and procurement risk for agent vendors
- **Records and accountability**: retention and eDiscovery for agent memory and outputs, evidence packs for regulators, liability and the Sponsor pattern, insurance
- **Sovereignty**: deployment spectrum (managed API to air-gapped), classification-based routing, localization requirements

## Challenged-default candidates

AI governance platforms vs extending GRC tooling; sovereign cloud offerings vs region pinning with contractual controls. Each track proposes its final list at kickoff.
