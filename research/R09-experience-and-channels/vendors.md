# R09 Experience and Channels: vendor map

As of 2026-08-19. Vendor capability claims are vendor-published unless third-party sourced. Re-verify quarterly.

| Vendor / product | Category | Maturity (Aug 2026) | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Contact-centre platforms (CCaaS category) | Channel and workforce infrastructure | Mature | Analyst rosters barely overlap with internal agent platforms |
| Conversational AI platforms | Customer-facing agents | Mature category, evaluated separately | Most vendors also claim employee-experience coverage |
| Customer-service knowledge management | Grounding for service agents | Inaugural analyst category, Aug 2026 | The market conceding that knowledge quality is decisive |
| Suite-embedded service agents | Unified customer and employee service | Shipping 2026 | The counter-argument to separation [vendor] |
| Outcome-priced service agents | Commercial model | List prices published | Roughly one to two dollars per automated resolution against a six-to-twelve dollar human comparator [vendor pricing] |
| Agentic commerce protocols | Buy-side reachability | One beta, one announced Jan 2026 | Machine-readable feeds and programmatic checkout are the enterprise requirement |

Agent-washing watch: resolution rates quoted without a definition of resolution; containment presented as resolution; voice demos in quiet rooms.
