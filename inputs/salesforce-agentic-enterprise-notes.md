# Salesforce "Agentic Enterprise" positioning: observed notes

Notes from Salesforce presentation slides observed in August 2026. **All claims below are vendor-published positioning**, recorded as input for the Phase 7 vendor analysis, where they are tested against the neutral question bank. No slide images are committed.

## Observed positioning claims

- Headline claim: "Only Salesforce connects these 6 systems in one Agentic Enterprise", the six being CRM, productivity, data lake, ERP, collaboration, hyperscaler, "federated and harmonized with Data 360"
- Tagline: "Determinism + Probabilism = Trusted Customer Intelligence"
- Stack presented as four bands: Interface (including Slack, Teams, ChatGPT, Copilot, Lightning, a "Claudeforce" entry, and a headless interface), Agency (Agentforce with named agents plus customer and partner agents), Action (Customer 360 clouds), Trust (Agentforce 360: trusted context via Informatica/Data 360/Tableau, agent builder, MuleSoft for action, Informatica MDM for governance, permissions and trusted services for security)
- A "trust layer with zero data retention" claim spanning model partners (Anthropic, Google, Hugging Face, NVIDIA, OpenAI)
- A named agent family marketed as "a trusted family of agents": Hunter (prospecting and sales), Piper (lead qualification), Casey (help), Paige (employee service), Marshall (supply chain), plus newer Felix (field), Margo (marketing), Archer (deal strategy), Carter (shopping)

## Why recorded

- The "only Salesforce" exclusivity claim is the program's worked example of neutralizing vendor narrative through the question bank (decision D008 context)
- The named-agent family is a useful specimen of productized presence-style agents anchored on CRM data gravity
- The determinism-plus-probabilism framing independently echoes the deterministic boundary principle, which strengthens the case that the principle is industry-converged
