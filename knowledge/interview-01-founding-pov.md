# Interview 01: founding POV

Status: captured conversationally 2026-08-19; answers synthesized into the published baseline chapters. Full capture held privately per the working protocol.
Purpose: capture the maintainer's vision, definitional tests, and priorities before the Phase 1 essay is written. Answers are recorded verbatim (lightly edited) and cited by findings.

## Questions

1. The bottling plant story: which visit was it, roughly when, and what exactly did you observe? What did the remaining humans actually do all day, and what skills made them valuable there? This story opens the guide, so detail matters.

2. Your definitional test: looking at a real enterprise, what observable evidence would make you say "this one is agentic"? What is the single most common false positive (things that look agentic but are not)?

3. From your client experience, which departments agentify first and why? Which department do you believe is hardest, and what makes it hard (data, risk, culture, economics)?

4. What have you personally seen fail in enterprise AI adoption so far? Name the patterns (anonymized): what was promised, what broke, what the post-mortem concluded.

5. Where do you stand today on build vs buy for the agent platform layer, and what evidence would change your mind in either direction?

6. Utilities specifically: what makes utilities different for agent adoption? Rank the constraints (OT boundary, regulation, workforce profile, asset intensity, safety culture) and give one example where an agent should never be allowed, in your view.

7. The determinism line: name the enterprise functions where you would never accept probabilistic behavior, and the ones where people wrongly demand determinism at high cost.

8. The reader: who exactly is page one written for (CIO, chief architect, platform lead, transformation lead)? What decision should the guide help them make in their first hour with it?
